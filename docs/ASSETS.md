# GoDash asset library and licences

Every third-party asset in the game is listed here with its source page and licence.
Rule for adding more: CC0 or an explicit commercial-use licence only, source URL recorded,
no reuploads of unknown origin.

## 1. Map data

| What | Source | Licence | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|---|
| Khon Kaen street network, lake, ponds, parks, railway, positions of real restaurants / shops / fuel stations | OpenStreetMap, via the Overpass API (`tools/osm_fetch.mjs`) | ODbL 1.0, © OpenStreetMap contributors | `src/shared/Config/Maps/KhonKaenData/*` (generated by `tools/osm_to_map.py`) |

The ODbL needs visible attribution: the phone shows "Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors" in Maps and Settings.
Only positions are taken from OSM. Business names are not copied; shop names in the game are our own.

## 4. 3D models (imported)

All from **Poly Haven**, licence **CC0 1.0**. Each was reduced in Blender (`tools/blender_prepare.py`), exported as GLB
(`assets/models/`), uploaded to Roblox as a Model asset and approved by moderation on 2026-11-14. The ids live in
`src/shared/Config/Models.luau`; the server loads them at start (`src/server/World/Assets.luau`) and the builders
fall back to their own simple parts when a model cannot be loaded (for example in an unpublished local place file).

| Key | Poly Haven model | Page | Triangles (source -> game) | Roblox asset id | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| chair | plastic_monobloc_chair_01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/plastic_monobloc_chair_01 | 3,356 -> 3,356 | 78916101840021 | seats at food carts and street tables |
| box | utility_box_01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/utility_box_01 | 4,404 -> 4,404 | 97338872927397 | roadside electrical cabinets |
| plant | potted_plant_04 | https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_04 | 8,929 -> 6,196 | 128599174038460 | potted plants in front of shops (scaled up 4.4x) |
| trash | metal_trash_can | https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_trash_can | 13,960 -> 6,995 | 82890708093077 | bins |
| aircon | exterior_aircon_unit | https://polyhaven.com/a/exterior_aircon_unit | 18,986 -> 6,000 | 77575198154795 | condensers on shophouse walls (every third unit) |
| manhole | water_manhole_cover | https://polyhaven.com/a/water_manhole_cover | 6,301 -> 1,997 | 132503814450919 | manholes on the road |
| cart | CoffeeCart_01 | https://polyhaven.com/a/CoffeeCart_01 | 27,659 -> 9,998 | 136492442494037 | street food carts |
| shrub | shrub_03 | https://polyhaven.com/a/shrub_03 | 8,287 -> 7,996 | 115943909801276 | loaded but not placed yet (too sparse to read as a bush) |

Checked and **not used**: `jacaranda_tree` (3.86 M triangles; still 77 k after the strongest reduction, over Roblox's
per-mesh limit) and `street_lamp_02` (a 1.7 m garden lamp, not a street light). Trees, vehicles, buildings and NPCs
are still built by our own code. A lighter CC0 tree source is still needed.

Rejected sources: Roblox Creator Store "free models" for trees and props are mostly reuploads with no verifiable
licence, which does not meet the rule at the top of this file.

To add another model: `node tools/polyhaven_models.mjs <id>`, add it to `BUDGET` in `tools/blender_prepare.py`, run
Blender, upload `assets/models/<id>.glb` through Open Cloud (`POST /assets/v1/assets`, assetType Model; needs the
`asset:read` / `asset:write` MCP permissions and `assets/models` as an approved upload directory), then add the asset
id to `Config/Models.luau`.

## 6. Radio music and animation

- Radio (`Config/Radio.luau`): 11 tracks from the Roblox Creator Store music library published by
  "DistrokidOfficial", which Roblox licenses for use in experiences. Outside links (YouTube etc.) cannot be played
  in Roblox.
- Pedestrian walk: Roblox's default R15 walk animation (`rbxassetid://507777826`).
- Dog bark: **no sound yet**. Every bark found in the Creator Store was a user upload with no verifiable licence
  (several were clips from other games), so the dog shows a bark bubble instead.
